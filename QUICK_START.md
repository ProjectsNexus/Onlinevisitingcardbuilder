# CardLink - Quick Start Guide

## 🚀 Getting Started

### 1. Create an Account
1. Visit the app homepage
2. Click **"Sign Up"**
3. Enter your name, email, and password
4. Click **"Sign Up"** button
5. You'll be redirected to your dashboard

### 2. Create Your First Card
1. Click **"Create New Card"** button
2. Browse and select a template from 12+ designs
3. Fill in your information:
   - Name (required)
   - Job Title (required)
   - Company (required)
   - Email (required)
   - Phone, website, address (optional)
   - LinkedIn and Twitter URLs (optional)
4. Click **"Save Card"**

## 🎨 Customizing Your Card

### Change Colors
1. In the card form, click the **"Custom Colors"** tab
2. Use color pickers or enter hex codes
3. Customize:
   - Primary Color (main background)
   - Secondary Color (accents)
   - Text Color
   - Background Color
4. Preview updates in real-time

### Edit Card Layout (Drag & Drop)
1. While editing a card, toggle **"Edit Layout"** ON
2. **To move elements:**
   - Click and drag any text or icon
   - Drop in desired position
3. **To resize text:**
   - Click on an element
   - Use the font size slider below
   - Adjust between 8px and 48px
4. Toggle **"Edit Layout"** OFF to see final result

## 📤 Sharing Your Card

### Generate QR Code
1. Open your card in edit mode
2. Click **"Generate QR"** button
3. In the modal:
   - View the QR code
   - Copy the shareable link
   - Download QR as PNG image
4. Anyone scanning the QR code can view your card

### Share Via Link
1. Click "View" on any card
2. Click **"Share"** button
3. Link is copied to clipboard
4. Share on social media, email, messaging apps

## 📊 Track Performance

### View Analytics
Your dashboard shows:
- **Total Cards**: Number of cards you've created
- **Total Views**: How many times your cards were viewed
- **Total Shares**: How many times cards were shared
- **Total Downloads**: Download count

Each card also shows individual stats.

## ✏️ Managing Cards

### Edit a Card
1. Find the card in your dashboard
2. Click the **Edit** button (pencil icon)
3. Make your changes
4. Click **"Save Card"**

### Delete a Card
1. Find the card in your dashboard
2. Click the **Delete** button (trash icon)
3. Confirm deletion
4. Card is permanently removed

### View Public Page
1. Click **"View"** on any card
2. See how others will see your card
3. Test share and download features

## 🆘 Common Issues

### "Generate QR" button disabled
**Solution**: Save your card first, then generate QR code.

### Elements overlapping after dragging
**Solution**: Toggle edit mode ON and reposition elements with more spacing.

### Card not showing in public view
**Solution**: Make sure card is saved. Check the URL has correct card ID.

### Can't login
**Solution**: 
- Check email and password are correct
- Try password reset (if implemented)
- Clear browser cache and try again

### Changes not saving
**Solution**: 
- Check internet connection
- Look for error messages
- Try refreshing and editing again
